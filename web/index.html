Pixel Control

See and control your phone from this computer, over USB.

  1. Plug your Pixel into this computer with a USB cable.
  2. Click Connect phone, pick your Pixel in the list, then click Connect.
  3. On the phone, tap Allow on “Allow USB debugging?” — tick Always allow from this computer so you only see it once.

After the first time, it connects by itself whenever you open the app with the phone plugged in. First time? USB debugging must be on — .

Setting up your Pixel (one time)

  1. On the phone open Settings → About phone and tap Build number 7 times until it says you’re a developer.
  2. Go to Settings → System → Developer options and turn on USB debugging.
  3. Plug the phone in, click Connect phone, choose it, and tap Allow on the phone.

Using it

  • Click to tap, drag to swipe, mouse wheel to scroll. On a touchscreen you can pinch and use two fingers.
  • Right-click or Esc = Back. Middle-click = Home.
  • Type on your keyboard to enter text in the phone. Ctrl+V pastes what you copied on the computer. Text you copy on the phone lands in the computer’s clipboard.
  • Drag files onto the phone screen to copy them to its Download folder. APK files are installed instead.

If it won’t connect

  • “In use by another program”: close Android Studio, scrcpy or anything else using adb (or run adb kill-server), then unplug and replug the phone.
  • Pixel not in the list: check USB debugging is on, try another cable or port, and unlock the phone.
  • Stuck at “tap Allow”: unlock the phone and look for the prompt. If it never shows, unplug, replug, and in Developer options try Revoke USB debugging authorizations.

Sound stays on the phone. Pixel Control uses the open-source scrcpy and Tango projects.
